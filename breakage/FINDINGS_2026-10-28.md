# What breaks: frozen RoboJEPA, 2026-10-28

## 1. Change-weighted scoring (RoboCasa pretrain bench, 3,000 anchors x 64 candidates)
Score = negative latent L1 to the goal over all patches (l1, the standard scorer) or only over the top q of patches
ranked by current->goal latent change (cw_goal{10,25,50}) or by predicted change (cw_pred25).
Script: probe/score_cw.py; analysis: breakage/analyze_cw.py; results: breakage/cw_results.json.
The l1 column reproduces the published scores exactly (max abs diff 0.0).

| size | scorer | overall rho | fine rho | regret cm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 22M | l1 | .746 | .359 | 5.68 |
| 22M | cw_goal50 / 25 / 10 | .717 / .682 / .631 | .327 / .288 / .239 | 6.56 / 7.43 / 8.75 |
| 22M | cw_pred25 | .724 | .331 | 6.39 |
| 1B | l1 | .789 | .418 | 4.99 |
| 1B | cw_goal50 / 25 / 10 | .754 / .712 / .653 | .361 / .306 / .260 | 6.02 / 7.18 / 8.30 |
| 1B | cw_pred25 | .753 | .356 | 6.01 |
| 8B | l1 | .788 | .409 | 4.98 |
| 8B | cw_goal50 / 25 / 10 | .754 / .712 / .653 | .356 / .301 / .257 | 6.09 / 7.05 / 8.11 |
| 8B | cw_pred25 | .751 | .350 | 6.08 |

Restricting the score to changing patches hurts at every size, monotonically in how hard it restricts. The
resolution floor is not the static background diluting the score; the action-relevant signal in these latents is spread
over the frame, and the predictor itself does not resolve fine differences.

## 2. Real video: DROID holdout (REAL lab, 256 clips, 16 frames, teacher-forced next-frame latent L1)
Script: probe/eval_droid_action_id{,_v2}.py; results: results/droid_action_id{,_v2}/ on Isambard.
Mean true translation per world-model step: 3.9 cm.
- Picking the true action sequence out of 16 (15 from other episodes): top-1 0.996-1.0 for every checkpoint (chance .0625).

Coarse action understanding on real video is solved: wrong episode, wrong direction, missing rotation, wrong gripper,
wrong timing are all detected. Magnitude is where it breaks: a 10% change in speed (~4 mm per step) is near chance
for the 22M models, the same few-millimetre floor as the RoboCasa ranking bench. More training (10k -> 150k) and more capacity (22M -> 1B) both move it: x0.9 goes .48 -> .56 -> .79, so on real
video the 1B model resolves ~4 mm/step differences 79% of the time (accumulated over ~15 steps). 8B not yet measured.
Caveat: scores sum over ~15 teacher-forced steps with true history frames, so this is easier than single-step ranking.
